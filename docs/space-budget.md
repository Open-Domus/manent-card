# Space budget

Generated by `tools/space_budget.py` from the model in `mechanical/`. Lengths in millimetres.
Part sizes come from `parts/parts.yaml`; several are still unverified there, and the cells
are trade size codes with estimated capacities, not a supplier's drawing.

## Rules the model applies

- Nothing lies on the cell. The board is a strip along one long edge, the cell takes the rest,
  and a rib of the frame 0.8 wide runs between them.
- Through the board, from the phone outwards: back skin, gap, parts, board, adhesive, front skin.
  Every part is on the face of the board towards the phone. The other face lies under the front
  skin and carries only the four contact pads.
- Through the cell: back skin, adhesive, cell, gap, front skin.
- Cell: 8% thickness growth allowed for; 0.2 around it; 1.5 beyond its body for the tabs.
- The microphones are bottom-port parts: their sound comes through the board from the front.
  They are at least 40 apart.
- Every part the card decides has a place on the board and none overlaps another or a keep-out (antenna
  corner, light window, microphone gaskets). The two QFN chips keep 0.3 clear.
- The other parts are counted here by area, each with 0.15 of spacing, and may take at most 50% of the
  free board. Their actual places are in the board file; the tracks are routed there, not checked here.
- Nothing here checks stiffness: whether the two skins keep a bent card from bending the cell.

## Bending

A load of 100 N in the middle of the card, held at two lines 70 apart: somebody sitting on it, gently.
"Together" is the two skins working as one beam, which needs them glued to the frame and the rib all
round without slipping. "Alone" is each skin bending by itself. The truth is between the two, and
nobody has asked a cell maker what radius a pouch tolerates.

| Skins | Together: sag | radius | Alone: sag | radius |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| two aluminium skins | 0.08 | 4.9 m | 9.07 | 0.04 m |
| aluminium back, plastic front | 0.4 | 1.0 m | 16.29 | 0.03 m |

Glued well, two aluminium skins bend about five times less than one. Not glued, neither is of any
use: the joint between skins and frame is the part of the shell that matters most.
